About Web2Fig

A practical bridge between the browser and the canvas.

Web2Fig is a focused Chrome extension for designers and front-end teams who want a fast, editable starting point in Figma from a live webpage.

One purpose

Web2Fig has one purpose: when you ask it to, it reads the visible structure and presentation of the current webpage and creates a Figma-compatible clipboard payload. It does not change the page, inject advertising, or act until you click the extension.

Built for editable work

A screenshot is useful for reference. Web2Fig aims to go further by translating page structure, computed styles, text, vectors, and image assets into a payload Figma can paste as editable layers. Results vary with the web platform: proprietary embeds, protected media, browser-internal pages, and some advanced visual effects cannot always be reproduced exactly.

Local by design

The extension has no account system, cloud backend, analytics, or telemetry. It processes the page you select in your browser and writes the result to your local clipboard. Read the full Privacy Policy for the precise data-handling details.
